# Adventure Racing Gear Guide

<br/>

## Introduction

Gear can make or break your race. Too much slows you down; too little leaves you unprepared. This guide covers mandatory safety equipment, discipline-specific gear, and organization strategies.

**Key Principle**: Read the race-specific gear list carefully. Requirements vary by duration, location, and season. Every item must pass gear check before race start.

<br/>

## Mandatory Safety Equipment

**Emergency Shelter**: Space blanket (reflects body heat, packs small)

**Signaling**: Whistle (pealess design), cell phone (waterproof case, for emergencies only)

**Light Sources**: Headlamp with spare batteries (300+ lumens), bike lights for biking sections

**First Aid**: Basic kit with bandages, gauze, tape, pain relievers, blister supplies

**Tools**: Knife or multi-tool for repairs and emergencies

**Water**: Purification tablets or filter for treating natural water sources

**Location-Specific**: Bear spray (bear country), snake bite bandage (where required)

<br/>

## Navigation Gear (Mandatory)

- **Compass**: Baseplate orienteering compass (Suunto, Silva, Brunton)
- **Map case**: Waterproof, clear, with lanyard
- **Permanent marker**: Waterproof ink for plotting coordinates
- **UTM plotter**: Optional tool for plotting coordinates

See [Navigation Guide](/content/navigation.md) for detailed techniques.

<br/>

## Clothing and Footwear

**Clothing Rules**: Avoid cotton (stays wet, causes hypothermia). Choose synthetic or merino wool (wicks moisture, dries fast).

**Mandatory Items** (varies by race):
- Waterproof jacket (breathable shell, not poncho)
- Long sleeves/pants (sometimes required)
- Gloves (lightweight for biking, warm for cold)
- Hat (sun protection or warmth)

**Footwear**:
- **Trail running shoes or light hiking boots**: Most important gear decision—must fit well and be broken in
- **Socks**: Non-cotton, bring 2-3 spare pairs, change at transitions
- **Water shoes**: For paddling sections (secure straps)

See [Trekking Guide](/content/trekking.md) for foot care details.

<br/>

## Bike-Specific Gear

**Mandatory**:
- Mountain bike (off-road capable, well-maintained)
- Bike helmet (meets safety standards)
- **Repair kit**: 1-2 spare tubes, tire levers, pump/CO₂ inflator, multi-tool with chain breaker, quick links (2-3), patch kit

**Highly Recommended**: Spare derailleur hanger, tubeless plug kit, duct tape, zip ties, chain lube

See [Biking Guide](/content/biking.md) for complete information.

<br/>

## Paddling Gear

**Mandatory**:
- **PFD** (Personal Flotation Device): Often race-provided, must be Coast Guard approved, worn at all times on water
- **Helmet**: Required for whitewater
- **Paddle**: Sometimes provided by race

**Recommended**: Dry bags (keep gear dry), throw bag (rescue rope)

See [Paddling Guide](/content/paddling.md) for comprehensive information.

<br/>

## Hydration, Nutrition, and Packs

**Hydration**: 
- Hydration bladder (2-3L) or water bottles
- Sprint races: 1-2L minimum
- 12-hour: 2-3L + refill plan
- 24+ hour: 2-3L + purification + refills

**Food Storage**: Easy-access pockets, pre-portioned in ziploc bags (prevents soggy food)

See [Nutrition Guide](/content/nutrition.md) for detailed strategies.

**Backpacks**:
- Sprint (2-4 hrs): 10-15L pack or vest
- 12-hour: 15-20L pack
- 24+ hour: 20-30L pack
- Look for: hydration compatible, multiple pockets, comfortable straps, waterproof

**Pack Organization**:
- Top/side pockets: Snacks, map, headlamp, phone
- Middle: Extra clothing, first aid, repair kit, food
- Bottom: Emergency gear, backup items
- Outside: Trekking poles, helmet (when not using)

<br/>

## Recommended Optional Gear

**Performance**: Trekking poles (reduce knee impact, improve balance), GPS/altimeter watch (track pace/elevation, not substitute for compass), sunglasses, sunscreen, lip balm

**Comfort**: Anti-chafe products (Body Glide), blister prevention (Leukotape, moleskin), spare socks and clothing in transition bins

<br/>

## Transition Area Organization

Store discipline-specific gear in bins at transition areas:
- Label clearly (team name, contents)
- Pack in reverse order (first-needed on top)
- Waterproof important items
- Create checklist for each bin

Contents: Bike gear, paddling gear (PFD, dry bags), spare clothing/socks, extra food/water, repair supplies

See [Transition Areas Guide](/content/transition-areas.md) for strategies.

<br/>

## Weight Management and Multi-Use Strategy

**Weight Penalty**: Every ounce matters over 12-24 hours. Unnecessary gear causes fatigue and slows pace.

**Reduce Weight**:
- Choose lightest mandatory gear
- Eliminate duplicates
- Use multi-use items (buff = hat + gloves + face mask)
- Repackage food
- Share gear within team when allowed

**Don't Cut**: Mandatory safety gear, essential repairs, adequate food/water, warm/dry layer

**Multi-Use Examples**: Buff (headband/hat/mask), duct tape (repairs/first aid), zip ties (bike fixes), trekking poles (balance/crossings), dry bags (waterproof/pillow)

<br/>

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Overpacking**: Carrying too much "just in case"—stick to mandatory + essentials only
2. **Untested gear**: Using new gear on race day—test everything in training
3. **Poor quality critical gear**: Cheap headlamps die, shoes fall apart—invest in reliability
4. **Poor organization**: Can't find items quickly—organize logically, practice transitions
5. **Inadequate waterproofing**: Wet everything—use dry bags and ziploc bags
6. **Ignoring foot care**: Blisters end races—good shoes, socks, prevention supplies
7. **Not reading gear list**: Missing items = penalties/DQ—check off every item
8. **Wrong gear for conditions**: Check weather, bring layers, plan worst-case

<br/>

## Quick Gear Checklist

**Sprint Race (2-4 hours)**:
- Safety: Whistle, emergency blanket, headlamp + batteries, first aid, knife, cell phone
- Navigation: Compass, map case, marker
- Clothing: Waterproof jacket, trail shoes (broken in), 2 socks, hat, sunglasses
- Bike: Mountain bike, helmet, spare tube, pump/CO₂, multi-tool, tire levers, quick links
- Paddling: PFD, water shoes
- Hydration/Food: 2L water, snacks for 4-6 hours
- Pack: 10-15L

**Add for 12-Hour**: Backup headlamp/batteries, extra warm layer, gloves, more food (12-15 hrs), water purification, 15-20L pack, spare socks, tubeless plug kit

**Add for 24+ Hour**: Emergency blanket per person, extended first aid, fire starter, extra batteries, full clothing change, comprehensive bike repairs, backup compass, increased food

<br/>

## Summary

Balance preparation with weight. Prioritize mandatory safety items, invest in quality for critical gear (shoes, headlamp, bike), and test everything before race day.

**Key Principles**:
- Read race-specific gear list carefully
- Test all gear during training
- Organize for quick transitions
- Multi-use items reduce weight
- Quality over quantity for critical gear
