# Transition Areas in Adventure Racing

Transition Areas (TAs) are where teams change disciplines and reset for the next leg. They’re also where teams lose huge chunks of time to chaos. The goal is simple: **leave the TA with the right gear, the right plan, and minimal stress**.

## What to do in every TA

- **Refuel**: real calories + refill water.
- **Reset**: socks/foot check, warmth, blister tape.
- **Repack**: next-leg gear accessible; last-leg gear contained.
- **Navigate**: plot/check the next leg before leaving.
- **Verify**: mandatory gear present.

## Pre-race: bins that make transitions easy

- Label bins by TA/leg (and your team name).
- Pack in “reverse order” (first-needed items on top).
- Keep a small “always” pouch (batteries, tape, wipes, spare socks).

> [!TIP]
> If you can’t find it in 10 seconds at home, you won’t find it in a TA.

## In-TA roles (parallel work wins)

Assign roles before you arrive:

- **Navigator**: plots/checks the next leg.
- **Food/water**: gets everyone eating and bottles refilled.
- **Gear/pack**: swaps shoes/layers, repacks, confirms mandatory kit.

Rotate roles across the race so one person doesn’t burn out.

Related: [Team Dynamics](/content/team-dynamics.md)

## A fast transition sequence

1. Quick status check: feet, cold, broken gear.
2. Start eating immediately.
3. Swap gear/layers.
4. Plot the next leg.
5. Final gear check + quick visual sweep.
6. Leave.

## Common mistakes

- Sitting down and “resting” (transitions balloon).
- Forgetting to eat because you’re busy.
- Rushing navigation and paying for it for hours.
- Leaving mandatory items behind.
